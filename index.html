Movements look leaderless from far away. Up close, there is always someone who carried the chairs, booked the venue, sent the invitations, believed first. For Ethereum Singapore, that someone is you.

🎵 Third Flavor — What Your Garden Grew in Us

You didn't just build a community, QZ — you opened your own doors to a wandering bard. You hosted me while I played music at the ArtScience Museum — my songs ringing through one of the most beautiful buildings on earth, because you made a place for them.

Every time one of those songs plays somewhere in the world — and they play now, on a little trail of music across my whole universe — Singapore is inside them. QZ and ED are inside them.

That is what a real community does: it doesn't just host events. It hosts people. It gives a musician a stage, a studio, and a reason to keep singing.
